Contact

For any question, bug report, or feature idea, email liberumaviss@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version and iPhone model so we can reproduce the issue faster.

Frequently asked questions

Is Retro Racer 88 free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions. The full game is free forever.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Retro Racer 88 does not require an internet connection to play, and it never sends data to any server.

How do I reset my high score?

Open the in-game Settings and tap Reset. This clears local high scores, achievements, and daily challenges.

The sound is silent on my iPhone.

Check the silent switch on the left side of your iPhone. The game uses native iOS audio, so it plays even when the switch is on, but if you have muted the app in Control Center or the system volume is at zero, audio will not play.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings inside the game and tap the language flag. English, Deutsch, and Türkçe are available.

Which iPhones are supported?

iPhone SE (2nd generation) and newer, running iOS 15 or later.

How do I unlock all cars?

All four cars are available from the start — Classic Racer, Sports Car, Monster Truck, and Motorcycle. Pick your favorite from the home screen.

What are the road themes?

The game cycles through six road themes every 30 levels: day, forest, desert, night, rain, and snow. You can also lock a theme from the home screen by picking a specific one instead of Auto or Random.

Report a bug

Send an email to liberumaviss@gmail.com with a short description of what happened and, if you can, a screenshot or screen recording. All bug reports are read by a human.

Feature ideas

Have an idea for a new car, boss, theme, or mechanic? Send it in — Retro Racer 88 is a solo indie project and player feedback shapes what gets added next.
